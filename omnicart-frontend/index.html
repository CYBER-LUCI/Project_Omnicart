Platform Features

What buyers and sellers get.

Multi-Seller Storefront

Each seller manages their own product catalog, stock levels, and pricing from a dedicated dashboard. Buyers see a unified product listing across all sellers.

Best Value & Fair Pricing

Competitive, transparent rates calibrated across demand and stock availability so you always get authentic pieces at true market value.

Multi-Category Catalog

Products organized across Electronics, Clothing, Home, Kitchen, and more. Filters by category, price range, and availability. Full-text and visual image search supported.

Checkout and Payments

Supports UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery. Order summary with address management, itemized totals, and delivery status tracking.

Seller Dashboard

Sellers can track revenue, units sold, low-stock alerts, and order fulfillment from a single dashboard. Inventory and pricing controls for each listed product.

GSTIN Verified Sellers

All merchant accounts include GSTIN verification badges. Buyers can see the seller identity and tax registration on every product page before purchase.
